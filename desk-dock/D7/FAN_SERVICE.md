# Fan service

Remove the laptop, lift the grille up its inclined rails, then lift the fan out of the open-top pocket. Refit the fan before sliding the grille down to its bottom stop. The grille's upper return closes the fan pocket. The frame rests on a lower ledge and is guided by the pocket and two front lips; the factory fan holes are unused.

The slide follows the fan plane, 18 degrees from vertical. Full release needs approximately 140 mm of travel. Removing the laptop first is part of the service sequence because the inclined fan path approaches the laptop space. A fan lead needs a free service loop or an accessible disconnect inside the open duct. The connector does not pass through a small closed wire hole.

The **120 x 120 x 25 mm** reference fan fits a 121 mm pocket. Its suction face and the intake cavity stay at the established position. The additional thickness grows outward: the front guides, grille and rails move 10 mm along the fan axis, and the pocket and upper return deepen to enclose the full frame. The 7.5 mm gap between the back of the grille and the discharge face is preserved. The grille is 4 mm thick, with 1.6 mm bars on a 9 mm pitch. Its front face is flat for printing, with the upper return growing from that face.

Thickness is parameterized from a fixed suction datum. The retained fan-center parameters locate the original pose anchor; the actual 25 mm frame center is 5 mm outward of that anchor. At the retained 18-degree angle, the 10 mm axial increase adds approximately 9.51 mm of horizontal depth and 3.09 mm of height at the front retention features. Each grille runs with 0.6 mm side clearance until two small friction lands engage over the final 6 mm. Their nominal interference is only 0.05 mm per side. This is a coupon starting point: print the short rail/land interface in the intended PETG and tune it for smooth hand removal and resistance to vibration. CAD overlap at these two specified lands represents the intended elastic fit; no holding force has been established. There are no separate fan pins, nuts or screws.

The fixed rails are rooted into the pocket along their height. Their front lips and fan guide lips remain outside the 113 mm air aperture. All bars share the grille's initial print plane.
