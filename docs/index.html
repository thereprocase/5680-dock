01

Put the weight on its feet.

Full-height R2 cradle profiles form the outer plenum sides. Bolted front and rear ties brace the two ends. Confirm the laptop contact with the V4 coupons before the large cradle prints.

02

Make room for the air.

Two split plenums retain the source mouth openings. The 120 mm fan centers move 12 mm outward for nominal lid clearance; flat guards have integral 7.5 mm standoffs.

03

Design the print first.

Broad exterior faces lie on the bed. Supports sit under reachable internal features; small holes bridge between landings. Orca verifies the selected geometry and poses.

01 / ACCESS

Open the halves.

The split exposes seam tabs and captive-nut pockets for support removal and assembly. Seal the mating seams and fan-fastener interfaces as described in the instructions.

02 / ORIENTATION

A pose for every part.

Shells print on their broad outer sides, cavities upward. Guards lie exterior-face down; ties print with their length in the layer plane. Keep the supplied plate poses.

03 / FIT

Check the real contact.

The R2 cradle profile is retained. The reported seating problem still needs the V4 coupon trial; support the laptop independently during initial fit checks.
