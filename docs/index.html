Precision 5680 / D9 P5

Less dock.
More desk.

One frame, swappable contact. No metal fasteners.

Two cradle ends and a splice plate carry the laptop at an 8° lean on drop-in seat and fence pegs, with a combined seat-and-fence contact at the middle, so another machine only needs new pegs. Split plenums, fan guards and T-jointed ties join with printed pins and removable keys. Now printing in ASA.

Make it yours.

Native Orca projects for the Repro P1S, 0.4 mm nozzle. The kit is sliced in PETG; the parts on the printer are calibrated Polymaker PolyLite ASA. Geometry and toolpath checks passed; each printed plate is logged with its geometry hash.

01 / ACCESS

Open the halves.

The split exposes printed seam-pin tabs for support removal and assembly. External blind fan sockets preserve the air wall. Optional seam sealant addresses physical leakage.

02 / ORIENTATION

A pose for every part.

Shells print on their broad outer sides, cavities upward. Guards lie exterior-face down; ties and pins print with their length in the layer plane. Keys print flat. Keep the supplied plate poses.

03 / FIT

Check the real contact.

The R2 cradle profile is retained. The reported seating problem still needs the V4 coupon trial; support the laptop independently during initial fit checks.

D9 P5 / CHECKS / OPEN QUESTIONS

Inspect the
print evidence.

Fifty-nine valid solids and watertight meshes; no nominal part, laptop or fan interference. Sampled pin, key and peg motions are clear. Both plenums pass the closed-intake audit.
